Worlds

Four families. 14 distinct worlds.

Each generated galaxy seed links together 24 unique star systems. While your ship instruments categorize planets into general families, exploration reveals 14 distinct variations scattered across the stars, waiting to be logged.

Verdant

Thick atmospheres, open water, and local plant growth. Mild, stable environments to gather your first basic supplies.

Dust

Dry, arid barrens where falling sand shifts beneath your boots and shifting wind currents challenge your structures.

Frozen

Ice-locked landscapes where open water is frozen solid and severe environmental cold drains your spacesuit batteries rapidly.

Scorched

Volcanic ridges of baked stone, hot ash fields, and open magma pools. High-hazard terrain requiring heavy protective suits.

System Overview

Engines of Progression

From the moment you wake up stranded in the wreckage, your trajectory is entirely open-ended. Carve out a home in the crust, engineer advanced networks, or build your way back into deep space.

01 // INDUSTRY

Station-Based Industry

Progression is completely physical. You craft basic starter stations, place them down in the world, and interact with them to forge advanced materials. By linking machinery together with networks of ducts and pipes, you can route power, smelt raw ore, and completely automate your manufacturing setups and resource gathering.

02 // SPACEFLIGHT & ROVERS

Vessels of Infinite Scale

Vehicles are whatever you dare to create. You can spend your days wandering the soil of a single home world, forging hand-crafted Land Rovers to climb its peaks. Or, you can look upward and build a capital ship so gargantuan, so heavy, that it cannot be built on a planet at all—a drifting orbital mega-structure that you call home while exploring an endless galaxy, launching smaller, handmade shuttles down to alien shores to gather what you need before returning to the stars.

03 // BIOSPHERE EXTRACT

The Living Universe

Worlds are never empty laboratories; they are breathing, unpredictable wildernesses. Survive by casting lines into unmapped planetary waters, holding tight against the fierce, erratic pulls of deep-water creatures where success relies entirely on your own focus and raw human attention. Out on the ridges, you can face the alien wildlife, capturing and taming strange procedural beasts to run your conveyor paths and guard your outposts, turning the planet's threats into the very heartbeat of your home.

04 // BIOMASS // POT REFINERY

Campfire Cooking Matrix

Raw food yields negligible nourishment and risks wasting critical bars on sickness. To survive, combine up to five ingredients in a campfire pot to unlock real nutrition. Recipes remain hidden until discovered by hand, logging your history for future remakes. Cooked meals spike long-term saturation, freezing your hunger bar for deep exploration runs.

5x Identical Berries ➔ Simple Berry Mash
1x Fish + 1x Crop + 3x Berries ➔ Hearty Fish Stew
05 // SURVIVAL

Survival & Exploration

Every world introduces changing environmental hazards. Severe planetary cold can sap your spacesuit batteries rapidly, while scorched stones cook your armor layers. Surviving means adapting to unpredictable terrains where sand dunes slide under your boots, water shifts realistically to find its own level, and felled trees crash straight down to the ground.

06 // NETWORKING

Cooperative Galaxies

Explore completely alone, open a quick local co-op session, or run a stable dedicated server for friends joining from anywhere. Network traffic remains lightweight and optimized, ensuring a crisp, seamless connection as your crew builds and explores together across the universe.

The Sandbox

Every new game starts badly. What happens next is up to you.

An asteroid strike cut your descent short, leaving you stranded in a broken ship. Armed only with the supplies in your storage lockers and a handful of notes in your journal, you are completely free to chart your own course. Settle down, build a massive factory, or turn your ship into a home to travel the galaxy forever.

  1. Establish

    Rummage through the wreckage console, check your starting locker supplies, and look through your journal to get your bearings on the ground.

  2. Forge

    Build an anvil to shape glowing iron ingots blow-by-blow, and run pipes between your machines to move your materials automatically.

  3. Engineer

    Flip the heavy iron switches on your fuel generators, wire up solar panels, and keep your machinery running smoothly through the dark night.

  4. Build

    Assemble a brand-new hull, balance physical thrusters against the ship's center of mass, and break into orbit to travel amongst the stars.

  5. Travel

    Settle down into a life on your starting world, or map out a fuel pipeline to cross into deep space. The galaxy loop is endless and entirely yours.

After dark

Something is out there

Monsters come out at night. Every world has its own species of Night Stalker, and each one spits something different. Some of it is webbing that sticks you fast.

Then there's the Watcher: tall, thin and grey, with arms that hang past its knees and a face with nothing on it. It only moves when you aren't looking at it, and a tree trunk between you counts as not looking. It comes out at night near trees, and crumbles at dawn.
